## Mesej Setup (hantar sekali di awal chat)

```
I'm making a 2D animated horror story series for YouTube. The attached image is the MAIN CHARACTER reference.
For EVERY image I ask for in this chat, follow these rules:
- 16:9 landscape, cinematic composition, like a frame from an animated film
- Style: consistent high-quality 2D animated comic illustration, bold clean outlines, detailed hand-painted textures, dramatic cinematic lighting, atmospheric shadows, muted post-apocalyptic palette
- NO text, NO captions, NO watermarks, NO logos in the image
- NO blood, NO gore, NO visible wounds (keep it advertiser-friendly: dread through lighting, shadows and expressions)
- When the main character appears, keep him IDENTICAL to the reference image:

MAIN CHARACTER (Cal): a male survivor in his late 20s to early 30s, short messy black hair, light facial stubble, tired but alert eyes, slightly rugged face with a few subtle scars, athletic but lean build. Weathered brown survival jacket with a dark hooded layer underneath, dark charcoal cargo pants, worn dark tactical boots, fingerless gloves, a red cloth armband tied around his LEFT upper arm. Signature item: a battered black handheld tactical radio with an orange display, long antenna and worn tape strips, clipped to his chest strap or in his hand. Black digital wristwatch.

Recurring side characters (keep consistent whenever they appear):
- DEE: Black woman, 58, short grey natural hair, reading glasses on a cord, navy fleece under a yellow supervisor's vest, calm stern face
- MARCO: Latino young man, 19, skinny, curly black hair under a backwards cap, oversized grey hoodie, forklift-driver hi-vis vest
- RAY: heavyset white man, 47, truck driver, balding, grey stubble, red-and-black flannel shirt, trucker cap; his right hand wrapped in a dark-stained napkin
- LENA: woman in her 30s, ponytail, red gas-station uniform polo with a name tag reading "LENA" (the only text allowed)

Reply "Ready" and wait for my first scene.
```

## Prompt Scene

### Scene 1: Cold open (part01)
```
Scene 1. Close-up of Cal standing inside a dark industrial walk-in freezer. Frost on his stubble and eyebrows, his breath forming a thick cloud. He's hugging himself against the cold, wide alert eyes glancing back at a heavy silver freezer door behind him. His black digital wristwatch glows, showing 1:35. Cold blue lighting, rows of frost-covered pallets fading into darkness.
```

### Scene 2: The warehouse (part02)
```
Scene 2. Wide establishing shot of a huge frozen-food distribution warehouse at night, viewed from the parking lot. Orange sodium streetlights, a long row of six loading bay doors, a quiet highway in the background, a big blank sign on the building. Light mist on the ground. Calm before the storm, a lonely and ominous mood. No people.
```

### Scene 3: The late truck (part03)
```
Scene 3. Night. A big 18-wheeler truck reversing into a loading bay at the warehouse, headlights glaring, orange warning light flashing. The bay door is stuck half-open. RAY the truck driver is climbing down from the cab, looking pale and grey, holding his wrapped right hand against his chest. Cal stands nearby on the dock in the foreground, watching him with suspicion.
```

### Scene 4: The break room call (part04)
```
Scene 4. Interior of a small warehouse break room on a mezzanine floor, harsh flickering fluorescent light. RAY slumps in a plastic chair, shivering, wrapped in a grey moving blanket. DEE stands holding an old desk phone to her ear with a worried, frustrated face. MARCO hovers in the doorway, scared. Vending machine and a wall clock in the background.
```

### Scene 5: He stopped shaking (part05)
```
Scene 5. The same break room seen from outside through a glass panel. A figure lies perfectly still in the chair, fully covered by a grey blanket. DEE and MARCO stand at a distance in silence: Dee composed but grim, Marco turned away with his head down. Cold, quiet, tense atmosphere, long shadows.
```

### Scene 6: He's standing (part06, awal)
```
Scene 6. Low-angle shot from the bottom of metal mezzanine stairs inside the warehouse. At the top of the stairs stands RAY, a dark silhouette backlit by the break room light, the grey blanket sliding off his shoulders, his head tilted unnaturally as if sniffing the air. Deep shadows, terrifying stillness. No blood.
```

### Scene 7: The pallet jack (part06, akhir)
```
Scene 7. Dynamic action shot on the mezzanine stairs. Cal shoves a heavy yellow pallet jack between RAY and DEE, pushing Ray backwards down the steps. Dee is pressing her hand over her forearm sleeve, shocked. Cal's face is full of fear and determination, the radio swinging from his chest strap. Motion lines, dramatic lighting. No blood.
```

### Scene 8: They're coming in (part07)
```
Scene 8. View from inside the loading dock looking out through the half-open bay door. The truck's headlights glare into fog, and dozens of dark human silhouettes are walking slowly toward the building from the highway, all turning toward the light and sound. The orange warning light flashes across the wet concrete. Ominous, overwhelming dread.
```

### Scene 9: Run (part08)
```
Scene 9. Cal sprinting down a narrow aisle between towering warehouse racks stacked with boxes, looking back over his shoulder in panic. Ahead of him is a heavy silver freezer door with a small frosted window, cold mist spilling from under it. Behind him, shadowy figures turn the corner at the far end of the aisle. Strong perspective, motion blur.
```

### Scene 10: Not alone (part09)
```
Scene 10. Inside the freezer, cold blue light, rows of frosted pallets. Cal has fallen on the icy floor, propped on one elbow, terrified. Three metres away stands LENA in her red gas-station polo, frozen mid-step with her arms reaching toward him, ice crystals forming on her shoulders, eyelashes and ponytail. Breath fog fills the air. No blood.
```

### Scene 11: Cardboard and counting (part10)
```
Scene 11. Inside the freezer, cold blue light. Cal crouches with his back against a frosted pallet, wrapping flattened cardboard boxes around his legs with packing tape, his hands tucked into his armpits between strips. His eyes stay fixed on LENA, who stands frozen and motionless in the background of the aisle, ice growing on her collar like white feathers. Thick breath clouds, long cold shadows.
```

### Scene 12: The radio (part11)
```
Scene 12. Close-up of Cal huddled against a frosted pallet inside the freezer, flattened cardboard taped around his legs. His shaking, frost-covered fingers press the button on the orange-screened tactical radio clipped to his chest strap. The red armband on his left arm is stiff with frost. His black wristwatch reads 1:40. Exhausted, desperate eyes. Blue cold light.
```

### Scene 13: She moved (end of part11)
```
Scene 13. Extreme close-up of LENA's frozen face in cold blue light, frost covering her skin and eyelashes. A single drop of meltwater runs down her cheek on the side facing the camera, and one eye has just turned toward the viewer. Pure dread, dark vignette edges. No blood.
```

## Pembetulan (buat semula dalam chat yang sama)

**Masalah dalam scene08 dan scene09:** Dee dan Marco dilukis sebagai zombie yang mengejar Cal. Dalam cerita, mereka berdua masih hidup dan bersembunyi di pejabat. Marco pun bercakap dengan Cal melalui radio di hujung episod. Penonton akan rasa keliru. Lagi satu, scene08 sepatutnya tunjuk orang ramai datang dari highway, bukan pintu chiller.
